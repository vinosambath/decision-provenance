---
title: Design Review for Order Fulfillment UI
date: 2025-04-12
participants: [Ana Petrova, Wei Lin, Jon Baptiste]
---

Ana Petrova: Alright, let’s dive into this design review. Wei, I think you had the first update?

Wei Lin: Yeah, so I’ve been iterating on the dashboard UI. I've added a couple of new widgets based on the feedback from last time. I think it’s cleaner, but—

Jon Baptiste: —Cleaner how? Like, are we talking tidier or just less stuff on the screen?

Wei Lin: Good question. I mean, less clutter. I removed the old user stats that nobody was looking at. Instead, I swapped them for an order summary. 

Ana Petrova: I know we mentioned getting rid of the stats, but… it feels like we’re trimming too much fat. Can we still get some form of user engagement metrics?

Wei Lin: Ugh, fine. I can sneak that in somewhere, maybe as a hover-over tooltip. 

Jon Baptiste: Or we could make a second page. It'd be less intrusive. Just a quick click-through to get more detailed metrics if someone’s curious.

Ana Petrova: I like that! But does that push us further down the road on timing? You know how stakeholders feel about “extra features.”

Wei Lin: When are they not grumpy about features? I swear, it’s like they want everything, but as little as possible. 

Jon Baptiste: Right?! “Can you make it work without making it work?”

Ana Petrova: What do you mean? Isn’t that our job? 

Jon Baptiste: Touché. Okay, Wei, what else do we have?

Wei Lin: Well, next I want to talk about the notification settings. I’m torn on how to present them. Do we go with checkboxes or maybe a slider?

Ana Petrova: I vote checkboxes; they’re familiar. Sliders make me feel like I’m controlling a video game. 

Jon Baptiste: Sliders can be super intuitive if we design it right, though. Remember how we struggled with the volume controls in—what was that project? 

Wei Lin: Oh, don't remind me. I still feel bad for the poor QA team who had to test that nightmare.

Ana Petrova: QA always has it rough—such unsung heroes. 

Jon Baptiste: Heroes with less-than-stellar coffee an hour into the morning, if you ask me. 

Wei Lin: Speaking of coffee, how is everyone doing on the caffeine front? I might need a refill soon!

Ana Petrova: I’m good, but I had the weirdest almond milk latte this morning. Tasted like it was still in the carton.

Jon Baptiste: Almonds with commitment issues? Sounds like a typical Monday.

Wei Lin: Okay, back to business. So, checkboxes it is then. I'll mock that up before our next meeting.

Ana Petrova: Perfect, and let’s make sure we follow up on the stakeholder feedback to keep them in the loop about changes.

Jon Baptiste: Crystal clear. Just no more game controls, okay?

Wei Lin: No promises! I might just make one for fun. 

Ana Petrova: Oh lord, we're doomed. Let’s wrap this up before I start dreaming of sliders!

Jon Baptiste: Agreed! Seriously though, we’ll feel great once we clean this up. 

Wei Lin: Absolutely. Thanks, everyone!
