title: Design Review Meeting
date: 2025-06-18
participants: [Priya Nair, Sofia Torres, Wei Lin, Daniel Cho, Marcus Webb, Jon Baptiste]
---

Priya Nair: Alright, team, let’s get started. We’ve got our usual design updates today. Sofia, you want to kick us off with the new dashboard layout?

Sofia Torres: Sure! So, I’ve made some tweaks based on the last round of feedback. Mostly it’s just reordering some elements, trying to make things more intuitive.

Marcus Webb: Intuitive, huh? You mean, less like Grandma trying to figure out her email?

Jon Baptiste: (laughs) Right? Everyone’s got a tech-illiterate Grandma somewhere. 

Wei Lin: As long as the dashboard doesn’t require a tutorial to understand, I think we’re good.

Priya Nair: Good point. Let’s keep that in mind—ease of use is paramount. So, Sofia, where did you land on the color scheme?

Sofia Torres: I went with a lighter theme. Something more vibrant, but not too overwhelming. I’m thinking of adding some contrasting elements for the buttons to make them pop. 

Daniel Cho: Pop? Is that the technical term now? 

Jon Baptiste: (smirking) “Make it pop” is definitely in the design handbook somewhere. Right next to “best practices.”

Marcus Webb: And “synergy”! Don’t forget synergy! 

Wei Lin: The holy trinity of buzzwords. 

Priya Nair: (chuckling) Okay, jokes aside, did anyone work on user testing for the new design?

Daniel Cho: We did a small session. Feedback was mixed. Some loved the changes, others weren’t sure about the new navigation.

Sofia Torres: Ah, back to square one with navigation issues, huh? 

Wei Lin: Why is it always navigation? It’s like a curse. 

Jon Baptiste: Like that old video game where you had to escape a maze. Maybe we need a map feature for the dashboard?

Marcus Webb: Or a treasure hunt! “Find the button to check your orders!” 

Priya Nair: (sighs, half-laughing) All things considered, maybe we should avoid turning this into a game. 

Daniel Cho: Well, we could set up some ‘quests’ for training. “Complete your first order to unlock a badge.” 

Sofia Torres: (laughing) I’d like to see the engineers wear their badges. 

Wei Lin: I’d wear mine proudly! It might actually increase morale. 

Priya Nair: (smiling) Okay, okay. Let’s focus back. So, more testing for the navigation is needed, right? 

Daniel Cho: Yes, I think more feedback will help us fine-tune it. 

Jon Baptiste: When do we want to aim for that? Next week? 

Marcus Webb: As long as we avoid scheduling around lunch. Not all heroes wear capes, but they definitely don’t do user testing on an empty stomach. 

Priya Nair: Agreed. Let’s aim for late next week to collect feedback. How does that sound?

Sofia Torres: Sounds good to me! 

Wei Lin: Works for me too. 

Daniel Cho: Sure, add it to the calendar. 

Marcus Webb: Just remember, lunch first!

Priya Nair: Always a priority, Marcus. Alright, moving on...
