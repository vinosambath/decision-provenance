date: 2025-05-21
participants: [Sofia Torres, Jon Baptiste, Marcus Webb, Nadia Ibrahim]

Sofia Torres: Alright, team, let’s wrap this up. I think we’ve all agreed that CircleCI is out, right? The pricing doesn’t make sense for our usage.

Jon Baptiste: Definitely out. I did the math and unless we magically triple our deployments, we’d just be throwing money away.

Marcus Webb: Yeah, and honestly, I don’t see us scaling that much in the next, what, year? 

Nadia Ibrahim: Agreed, and if we’re honest, nobody wants to touch Jenkins again. The last time someone did that, we nearly had a meltdown.

Sofia Torres: Right! I mean, who wants to babysit Groovy scripts? It’s like entering a time warp back to 1995.

Jon Baptiste: Exactly! I still have nightmares about that. Though I might have accidentally deleted production once... just once!

Nadia Ibrahim: Oh, please! Don't remind us of your “accident.” 

Marcus Webb: Haha, who knew ‘accidentally hitting delete’ would make you team lead, right? 

Sofia Torres: Okay, okay, stay on target. So, are we saying GitHub Actions feels like our best bet now? 

Nadia Ibrahim: Honestly, it checks a lot of boxes. It’s straightforward, and we can set it up without a degree in Groovy.

Jon Baptiste: And integration with our codebase? Super smooth. I mean, we’re already in GitHub all day long, it’s just... it feels right, you know?

Marcus Webb: Totally! Plus, we’ve got plenty of flexibility with those workflows. 

Sofia Torres: Just a thought — do we want to discuss self-hosted runners at all? I know it’s not part of our immediate plan.

Nadia Ibrahim: You mean future-proofing? We can always come back to that once we need it, but yeah, I’d say at least a year until we cross that bridge.

Jon Baptiste: Plus, self-hosting means more maintenance, and ain't nobody got time for that right now.

Marcus Webb: Agreed. Let’s not bite off more than we can chew today. How about we commit to GitHub Actions and revisit this down the line?

Sofia Torres: Sounds like a plan! So, we’re good with GitHub Actions moving forward? 

Nadia Ibrahim: Definitely! I think we should draft a brief to get it rolling.

Jon Baptiste: Full steam ahead with GitHub Actions then! 

Marcus Webb: Alright, let’s do it! 

Sofia Torres: Awesome! Alright, team, meeting adjourned!
