title: Exploring Feature Flags Options
date: 2025-04-02
participants: [Omar Siddiqui, Grace Kim, Leila Haddad, Victor Nguyen]
---

Omar Siddiqui: Alright team, thanks for making time today. Let's dive into feature flags. I know we’ve been considering LaunchDarkly, but we should also weigh our other options carefully.

Grace Kim: Totally agree, Omar. I’m actually leaning toward LaunchDarkly right now, especially since I heard their per-seat pricing stays flat through next year, which could be a big win for us budget-wise.

Victor Nguyen: I mean, that’s a plus, but have we thought about building our own solution? I know it sounds daunting, but we have the talent.

Leila Haddad: Right, but remember, building our own flags could take ages—like, what did you say, Omar? A whole quarter? That's a lot of time to just be… what, waiting?

Omar Siddiqui: More or less, yes. We can’t just sit on our hands while we're waiting for something to be built. We need flexibility, not just a DIY project that drags on.

Grace Kim: Yeah, but if we go with LaunchDarkly, we lose some control. I mean… are we just going to pay them forever?

Victor Nguyen: That’s fair, but have you looked at Unleash? It’s self-hosted, which means we have control, but…

Leila Haddad: …nobody wants to run another stateful service. It’s like we’d just be adding more complexity, right?

Omar Siddiqui: Exactly! It’s a balancing act—too much complexity, and we’re back to square one. And if we pick Unleash, who’s going to manage it?

Grace Kim: Speaking of management, I do think LaunchDarkly’s UI is way smoother. My team loved it when I did a demo. Easier for Product to visualize.

Victor Nguyen: But is smoothness worth the cost? I just worry we get locked into these services without really considering our future needs.

Leila Haddad: Yeah, but if we don’t decide quickly, we won’t have time to implement anything for the next deployment. Isn’t that on the horizon?

Omar Siddiqui: Well, we can’t rush this either. We need the right solution. Maybe we should gather more feedback from Product?

Grace Kim: That might help clarify the targeting rules they need. If those rules are complicated enough, it could change our perspective on everything.

Victor Nguyen: What if we set up a quick survey? Capture what features are crucial for them? It could shed light on whether we need something robust or if soft options like LaunchDarkly for now would suffice.

Leila Haddad: I like that idea. Let’s avoid a decision just for the sake of it. Nobody wants to deal with the fallout if we choose wrong.

Omar Siddiqui: Agreed. It sounds like we need to gather more info before committing further. Let’s pencil in feedback collection for next week and reconvene after that.

Grace Kim: Sounds like a plan. I’ll start drafting some questions for Product.

Victor Nguyen: And I can do a little digging into Unleash’s documentation—see if my concerns about running it are justified.

Leila Haddad: Perfect! Teamwork makes the dream work, right? Or something like that…

Omar Siddiqui: Exactly! Let’s keep it rolling. Thanks, everyone.
